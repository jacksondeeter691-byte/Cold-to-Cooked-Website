# Cold-to-Cooked Reels Playbook

This is the 5-step method from the ReelFactory reel, applied to Cold-to-Cooked. It runs on our own free reel maker (`marketing/reels/`) instead of the $19/month tool.

## The method, in five steps

| Step | What the reel said | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Set up once** | Install animation tools, set up a voice, time captions to every word, learn good motion, check its own frames | Done: `marketing/reels/` renders word-timed motion reels from a short script file |
| **02 The problem** | Don't make ads for your app | Nobody searches for "Cold-to-Cooked". People search "what to cook with eggs and rice". Ads get skipped and answers get saved. |
| **03 List the questions** | Say your app tracks spending: "why am I always broke before payday?" Ten questions is ten reels | Every question below is one reel. The question is the hook. |
| **04 Give one useful thing away** | One useful thing, and then the app | About 70% of the reel is a real answer that works without the app. The app comes in the last third as the shortcut. |
| **05 One line** | One prompt makes the reel | One script file per reel, one command to render it |

**The call to action:** "Comment **COOK** and I'll send you the app." Comments push reach, and an automatic DM sends the App Store link. Set it up once with **ManyChat** (free plan, connects to Instagram): Automations → "User comments on your post or reel" → keyword `COOK` (plus `PROTEIN` and any others you use) → DM: *"Here you go 👨‍🍳 Cold-to-Cooked, snap your fridge and get recipes: [App Store link]. 3 free scans a day."*

## Shape of every reel (20–35 seconds)

1. **Hook (0–2s):** a bold claim or the question itself. Big text plus one emoji. No logo and no app name.
2. **The problem (2–6s):** show the situation the viewer is in.
3. **The useful thing (6–18s):** a rule, list or 3–4 step trick they can screenshot and save.
4. **The shortcut (18–28s):** "Or snap your fridge…" showing the app doing it in seconds.
5. **The CTA (last 4s):** "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the app."

## Posting plan

- **Volume beats perfection.** Post 1 reel a day for 30 days. Each one takes about 10 minutes once the script is written.
- **Post everywhere:** the same MP4 goes to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Upload to each one natively; don't share a link from one to another.
- **Sound:** the reels come with an AI voiceover and a background beat. You can also add a trending sound at very low volume inside the app for an extra reach boost. The captions carry the message with the sound off.
- **Caption:** each script has a `caption` field. Paste it and keep 3–5 hashtags.
- **Cover:** use the `-cover.jpg` the renderer writes, so the grid looks consistent.
- **Read the numbers after 48 hours.** Saves and shares mean the useful thing landed. Comments of the keyword mean the CTA landed. Make more reels like your top 3, rewrite the hooks on your bottom 3, and repost the best ones a month later with a new hook.
- **Pin** your best-performing reel to the top of your profile, and put the App Store link in your bio.
