Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Cold-to-Cooked ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and user ID when you create an account
  • Photos: Images you take or select from your photo library for ingredient scanning. These are processed to detect ingredients and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Health data: Nutrition goals and meal logs you enter, which may be synced with Apple HealthKit with your permission
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, collected via Mixpanel analytics
  • Purchase history: Subscription status managed via RevenueCat
  • Crash data: Technical diagnostic information collected via Sentry to help us fix bugs

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app's features
  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription
  • To personalize your experience (health goals, recipe suggestions)
  • To analyze app usage and fix technical issues
  • To respond to customer support requests

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — processes ingredient photos and generates recipes
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases
  • Mixpanel — app usage analytics
  • Sentry — crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Unsplash — food photography displayed alongside recipes

Apple HealthKit

If you grant permission, Cold-to-Cooked may read and write health data (calories, protein, water intake) to Apple HealthKit. This data is used solely to power the Health Tracker feature and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on servers hosted by Railway. We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and JWT authentication to protect your information.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@coldtocooked.app.

Children's Privacy

Cold-to-Cooked is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Revoke HealthKit permissions at any time via iPhone Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@coldtocooked.app.
